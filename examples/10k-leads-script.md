# Example: the $10K-a-month leads script

Built live on the 9/23 call. The point of this file is not the final script. It is the path: seven renditions of one hook. Seven is more than usual because we narrated every step live; three or four is typical.

Topic: posting content that turns into leads.
Pain it hits: "I'm posting but it's not turning into business."
Want ladder: started at "how to go viral" (views), moved down to "how to get leads" (leads), then to "$10K a month and unlimited leads" (money + leads).
Container: a top-question outlier ("The number one question I get asked is how to [X]").

## How the hook got better

**Rendition 1, the seed**
> The number one question I get asked is how do I generate more leads from content?

Container in place. Missing: a number, a stake, an open loop.

**Rendition 2, add a number and borrowed authority**
> The number one question I get asked is how can I make $10,000 a month from posting on Instagram?

Now it has a number ($10,000 a month) and borrowed authority (Instagram, the mechanism everyone knows). Still missing a clean open loop.

**Rendition 3, Claude's draft**
> The number one thing I get asked is, how do I turn videos into leads? Because viral videos that get zero leads are just free entertainment. So here's exactly what I do.

Better. But the second half has no punch. "Because viral videos that get zero leads are just free entertainment" feels like filler. Everything in the hook has to earn its spot.

**Rendition 4, bolt on a loop**
> The number one question I get asked is how to make $10,000 a month posting. So let me show you. It's actually really simple, as long as you do this one thing that almost nobody is doing.

Has a loop now, but it's cheesy and cliche. Needs to be more tangible and tease a mechanism.

**Rendition 5, tease the mechanism with a count**
> Every creator making $10,000 a month on Instagram is doing the same three things, but almost everybody misses the third one. So let me show you what it is.

Big jump. "Three things" plus "everybody misses the third one" is a real open loop. The problem: it's external. It's about other creators, not the viewer.

**Rendition 6, put it on the viewer's aspiration**
> If you want to make $10,000 a month posting to Instagram, you need to be doing these three things. But almost every single person misses the third one, and it's why they don't have any leads. So let me show you what it is.

Now the viewer is in the hook as the person who wants the outcome. Close. But the buyer's real dream is not just $10K. It's $10K **and** leads coming in.

**Rendition 7, the final hook**
> If you want to make $10,000 a month and have unlimited leads just by posting on Instagram, you need to be doing these three things. But almost every small creator misses the third one, and it's why they don't have any leads. So let me show you what it is.

All four parts:
- **Stake:** "it's why they don't have any leads"
- **Authority (borrowed):** Instagram, plus "these three things" implies a known system
- **Open loop:** "almost every small creator misses the third one... let me show you what it is"
- **Number:** $10,000 a month, three things

Could go more niche ("If you're a fitness coach and you want...") but keeping it broad keeps the audience big.

## The full script

**Beat 1: Hook (under 10s)**
> If you want to make $10,000 a month and have unlimited leads just by posting on Instagram, you need to be doing these three things. But almost every small creator misses the third one, and it's why they don't have any leads. So let me show you what it is.

**Beat 2: Descriptor (about 10s)**
> This is the number one question I get almost every day. People are posting online, but it's not converting into new business for them. They post content for a couple of months, then burn out and get discouraged. The thing is, they're almost there. They're just missing a few small things that can take their content from this to this. And to get there, you just have to follow [framework name]. Here's how it works.

Note: the "number one question" line moved here from the hook. In beat 2 it opens a second loop instead of slowing the hook down.

**Beat 3: The teach (about 35s)**
> [Pat's core teach goes here: three things, the third one being the one most people miss.]

Pat's idea from the call, to build out as thing number three:

- Most coaches already had a winner. They posted something that crushed, then never did it again because it felt like repeating themselves.
- The fix: when a post wins, redo it. Keep the format. Change the exercises, the scenario, the setting, or tweak the topic.
- Fitness example of the move: a viral "if you do 10 sit-ups, 10 squats, 10 pull-ups, this is what happens" video. Same format, new exercises, post it again.
- Why it works: more swings at a known winner beats a brand new idea every time. The top creators remix. The ones who aren't winning keep starting from zero.

Things one and two: Pat to fill in. Name the whole framework in two to four words before the steps.

**Beat 4: Positioning (about 5s)**
> [Pat's "it's not your fault" line. Pat's own shape from the call: "And it's not your fault. People taught you this back in 2022, and since the algorithm update it's all changed."]

**Beat 5: CTA (about 8s)**
> If you follow these, it's going to put you in the top 1% of creators and take you from making no money posting on social media to having endless leads coming in. So save this video, and if you want a full guide breaking down every step and how to apply it to your content today, comment [KEYWORD] and I'll send it over.

## What to steal from this

1. The seed was a container from an outlier, not a blank page.
2. Most renditions fixed one thing. Two were full rewrites (3 and 5), and both moved the hook forward, so a rewrite is fine when the structure is wrong.
3. The biggest jumps came from three moves: adding a count with a missed item ("three things, most miss the third"), flipping external to aspirational ("If you want..."), and naming the buyer's real want (leads, not just money).
4. Once the hook locked, the body took minutes.
