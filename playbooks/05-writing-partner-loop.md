# 5. The writing-partner loop (10-80-10)

**The split:**
- **10% you:** the concept, the topic, a rough hook. Brain dump. Messy is fine.
- **80% Claude:** drafts, options, structure, tightening.
- **10% you:** final polish in your words. Your sauce.

**Claude is a sparring partner, not the writer.** Most people let AI do the creative work. Flip it: you tell it what you want, it refines.

**Go section by section.** Hook first. Only the hook. Once it's nailed, the rest takes no time. Writing the whole script at once gives you too much to react to and nothing gets fixed.

**When you hit a blank, that's when you go to Claude.** "I like this angle but there's no open loop at the end. Give me three."

**Point it at your past winners.** "This worked in another video. How do we use that move here?"

**When a script lands, say "save the framework."** First script: an hour. Next one: 40 minutes. Eventually you just show up with a topic and one or two containers.

Skill: `script-writer`
