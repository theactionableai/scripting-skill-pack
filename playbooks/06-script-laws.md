# 6. Script laws and the kill list

**The eight laws:**
1. **Topic decides.** If nobody cares about the topic, no hook saves it.
2. **Open with a punch.** No setup, no throat-clearing. One breath.
3. **Hold the reveal.** The hook never gives the answer.
4. **Bold first, then fact-check.** Write it bold, then make every claim true.
5. **No defusals.** Don't cancel your own stake with "but don't worry".
6. **Connected beats, proof early.** Show a result or example by beat 2 or 3.
7. **The close matches the giveaway.** Say exactly what they get.
8. **Two strikes, back to the topic.** Rejected twice for something other than wording? The topic or claim is the problem, not the words.

**Kill on sight:**
- Vanity numbers (followers, downloads) as the hook
- "According to research..." opens
- Lines any account could say
- A second half that feels glued on
- No open loop
- Clever over blunt
- Four numbers where one would do
- Weak verbs (shared, posted, launched)
- Choppy fragments with no So / And / Because
- Half-committed takes
- Safe, down-the-middle statements

**Keep your own kill list.** Every time you reject something twice, write it down in your words in `my-context/voice.md`. Yours beats the generic list.
