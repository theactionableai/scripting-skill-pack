# 3. The 4-part hook

**The rule:** 80% of your scripting effort goes on the hook. It's beat 1, and it runs under 10 seconds.

**The four parts:**
1. **High-stakes sensational angle.** Something they could lose or win. Bold, not safe.
2. **Authority.** Borrowed (a platform, a name, a group they trust) or earned (your result).
3. **Open loop.** A question they need answered. Tease it, never give it.
4. **Specific number.** Something they can pin. Hooks without a number do worse.

**Put it on their aspiration, not outside them.**
- Weak: "Every creator making $10K a month is doing three things."
- Strong: "If you want to make $10K a month, you need to be doing these three things."

Then check you named what they really want. On the call, $10K a month wasn't enough. "$10K a month **and unlimited leads**" is what landed.

**How to get there:** write a rough one yourself, then go back and forth with Claude. Fix one part per round. Three or four renditions is normal.

**Quick checks:** Would a stranger stop on the first 10 words? Can you say it in one breath? Does it give away the answer? (It shouldn't.)
