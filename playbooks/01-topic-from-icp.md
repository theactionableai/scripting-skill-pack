# 1. Topic from ICP

**The rule:** every topic attaches to one thing your buyer feels. A pain, a burning question, a desire, or a transformation. No attachment, no video.

**The want ladder.** Nobody posts because they want views. Keep asking "and why do they want that?"

views -> leads -> money, easier sales, authority, ego

"How to go viral" is a views topic. "How to turn posting into clients" is the same idea a rung or two lower, and it sells. Go as low as you can.

**The stranger test.** Would someone in your buyer's world who's never heard of you care, in one sentence? You need two of: fresh (something changed), recognized (a name they know), felt stake (money, clients, time, status).

**Before you script, write this down:**
- Topic in one line
- The pain, question, desire or transformation, in their words
- The deepest rung it hits
- The one thing they could lose or win

Skill: `topic-ladder`
