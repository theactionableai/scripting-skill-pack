# 2. Swipe file and container remix

**The rule:** don't invent formats. Find ones already working and put your idea inside.

**What to save:** accounts **under 100K followers** with a reel **over 200K views**. Small accounts matter because their wins come from the format, not their fame. That's a format that hasn't been overplayed.

**Save all day.** Not just when it's time to film. When you sit down to script, the containers are already waiting.

**For each save, write down:** the hook word for word, the hook with the subject removed (that's the container), the beats, and why you think it worked.

**The remix.** Keep the format. Swap one thing:
- the topic
- the scenario
- the setting
- the exercise or example

**Your own winners count most.** Most coaches had a video crush and never did it again. Redo it. Change the exercises, keep the format. More swings at a known winner.

**Show up to every script session with:** a topic and one or two containers. That's the two hardest battles already won.

Skill: `swipe-file`
