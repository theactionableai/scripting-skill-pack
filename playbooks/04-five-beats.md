# 4. The five beats

| Beat | What | Time | Effort |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Hook | Stake, authority, loop, number | under 10s | 80% |
| 2. Descriptor | What's in it for me. Opens a second loop. | ~10s | 10% |
| 3. The teach | Name the framework, break it down | ~35s | 5% |
| 4. Positioning | It's not your fault | ~5s | 2.5% |
| 5. CTA | Save, send, or comment for the giveaway | ~8s | 2.5% |

Total: 50 to 70 seconds.

Effort is importance, not length. The first 20 seconds get about 90% of your energy. Some days you take a decent draft of beats 3 to 5 and roll with it.

**Loop cadence:**
- Open a loop before you close one.
- Never close two in a row.

**Don't perfect all five at once.** Get beat 1 great. Then beat 2. Keep the bones of the rest. You'll get faster at the later beats over time.

Skill: `beat-builder`
