# The partner loop: prompts that move each step

These are the kinds of things the user says, and what you do with each. Use them as patterns, not a script.

## Opening a session

User: "Pull up my scripting process. I want a new video on [topic]. Here is the outlier I want to follow: [link]."

You: confirm the topic in one line, name the pain or desire it attaches to, map the container from the outlier, then ask for their rough hook. Nothing else yet.

## Pointing at a container

User: "No, that's nowhere near it. I want to follow the framework in this outlier video, centered on its structure but with my concept."

You: write the outlier's structure as a fill-in skeleton (what each line does, with the topic slot empty), then fill the slots with their concept. Show both.

## Adding the stake

User: "What's the stake, the emotion, the transformation we can convey?"

You: list three: the pain right now in the buyer's words, the dream outcome with a number, and the identity they want. Ask which one leads.

## Filling a blank

User: "I like this angle. My only issue is there's no good open loop at the end. How do we add one?"

You: give three loop endings that each leave a different question open. Check `winners.md` for a loop that already worked and offer it as one of the three.

## Borrowing from a past winner

User: "Here's something that worked really well in another video. How can we use it here?"

You: name the move it used in one sentence (for example: "it promised three things and teased that most people miss the last one"). Then show the current hook with that move stitched in.

## Simplifying

User: "This feels cliche. Make it more tangible."

You: replace any abstract phrase with a concrete one: a number, a named thing, a picture. Cut any word that is not doing work.

## Turning it back on the viewer

User: "This is external. Put it on them."

You: rewrite so the viewer is the subject and the dream outcome is theirs. "Every coach making $X is doing..." becomes "If you want to make $X...".

## Moving on

User: "Cool, show me the rest of the script body."

You: write beat 2 only, then ask. Then beat 3. Keep each beat in its own message unless they ask for the full body in one go.

## Locking it

User: "Save the framework."

You: append to `my-context/saved-frameworks.md` using `save-the-framework.md`. Confirm in one line what you saved.
