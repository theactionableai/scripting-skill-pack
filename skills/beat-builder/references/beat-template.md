# Five-beat template

Copy this and fill it in order, one beat at a time.

```markdown
# [Working title]

Topic: [one line]
Pain or desire: [from icp.md]
Container: [outlier or saved framework it is built on]
Target length: [50 to 70 seconds]

## Loop map
- Beat 1 opens: [loop A, as a question the viewer is asking]
- Beat 2 opens: [loop B]
- Beat 3 closes: [B on the framework name, A on the last step, with steps in between]
- Beat 4 opens: [small loop, optional]
- Beat 5 closes: [with the giveaway]

## Beat 1: Hook (under 10s, 80% of effort)
[spoken words]
On screen: [text card / visual]

## Beat 2: Descriptor (about 10s)
[spoken words: name the pain, feel seen, open loop B]

## Beat 3: The teach (about 35s)
Framework name: [two to four words]
1. [step one, what + why]
2. [step two, what + why]
3. [step three, what + why]
On screen: [list ticking up / example / before and after]

## Beat 4: Positioning (about 5s)
[it's not your fault, blame the advice]

## Beat 5: CTA (about 8s)
[save / send / comment KEYWORD for the exact thing]
```

Read the whole thing out loud once at your normal speed. If it runs long, cut from beats 3 to 5, never from the hook's four parts.
