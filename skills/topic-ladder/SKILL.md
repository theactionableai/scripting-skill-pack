# Topic Ladder

A topic with no pain or desire attached is just information. Nobody stops for information. They stop because something in the video is about them.

## Load first

Read `my-context/icp.md` (default location `~/.claude/my-context/`). It holds the buyer, their pains in their own words, their desires and their burning questions. If it is blank, ask the user three questions before going on: who is the buyer, what is the number one thing that hurts, what do they want instead.

## Step 1: attach the topic to one of four hooks into the buyer

Every topic gets exactly one of these, written in the buyer's words where possible:

| Type | Question it answers | Example (fitness coach buyer) |
|---|---|---|
| Pain | What hurts right now? | "I post every day and get zero leads." |
| Burning question | What do they keep asking? | "How do I get clients from content?" |
| Desire | What do they want? | "Fully booked without paying for ads." |
| Transformation | Where do they want to go, from where? | "From $20K a month to $50K without working more hours." |

Pick one. A topic trying to hit all four hits none.

## Step 2: walk down the want ladder

Surface wants sit on top of deeper wants. Keep asking "and why do they want that?" until you hit the real driver. The deeper you go, the more the topic sells.

```
views          "I want my videos to get seen"
  leads        "I want people asking to work with me"
    money      "I want $10K, $50K, $100K months"
    easier sales   "I want buyers who already trust me"
    authority  "I want to be the name in my space"
    ego        "I want to be seen as someone who made it"
```

Your buyer rarely posts just for views. They post because they want more business. So "how to go viral" is a weak topic for a coach buyer. "How to turn posting into clients" is the same topic a rung or two lower, and much stronger.

Be honest about ego. People post because they want more opportunity and they want to be seen. Aspiration is allowed in the script.

## Step 3: the stranger test

Read the topic as one sentence. Would someone in the buyer's world who has never heard of the user care? It needs at least two of these three:

- **Fresh**: something changed recently (an update, a news item, a trend this week)
- **Recognized**: a name, platform or institution the viewer knows without being told (Instagram, a well-known coach, a big brand)
- **Felt stake**: money, time, clients, health, status, something they would be upset to lose

Evergreen topics can pass with one strong pain in the buyer's own words plus something you can show on screen.

## Output

Give the user this, and nothing else, then wait:

```
Topic: [one line]
Attached to: [pain / question / desire / transformation]: "[their words]"
Ladder rung: [the deepest want this hits]
Stranger test: [which two or three it passes]
Stake to lead with: [the one thing the viewer could lose or win]
```

If it fails the stranger test, say so plainly and offer one way to go lower on the ladder.

## Brainstorm mode

When asked for topic ideas, give five, each one line in the output format above, each attached to a different pain or desire from `icp.md`. Do not give more than five.
