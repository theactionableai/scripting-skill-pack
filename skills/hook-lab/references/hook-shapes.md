# Hook shapes

Fill-in shapes that have carried outlier videos. The subject is removed so you can drop in your own. Imitate shapes, not rules: rules describe averages, winners are outliers.

Always check the user's own `my-context/winners.md` first. A shape that already worked for them beats any shape here.

Fitness-coach fills are examples only. Swap in the user's real topic and numbers.

## Value and teaching shapes

**Aspiration + count + missed one** (the shape from the example script)
"If you want to [dream outcome with number], you need to be doing these [N] things. But almost every [person like the viewer] misses the [last] one, and it's why they [pain]. So let me show you what it is."
> If you want to sign 5 new clients a month from Instagram, you need to be doing these three things. But almost every coach misses the third one, and it's why their DMs are dead. So let me show you what it is.

**Top question**
"The number one question I get asked is [burning question]. Here's exactly what I do."
> The number one question I get asked is how do I get clients from content without running ads. Here's exactly what I do.

**Here's how to** (method)
"Here's how to [outcome] so [intensifier]."
> Here's how to turn one reel into a week of booked calls, so you never post into silence again.

**The reason your**
"The reason your [thing] [fails] is because [cause the viewer has not considered]."
> The reason your content gets views but no clients is because you're teaching the wrong half of your offer.

**If I had to start from zero**
"If I had to [outcome] from zero, this is exactly what I'd do."
> If I had to get my first 10 online clients from zero, this is exactly what I'd do.

**The ultimate artifact**
"This is the [checklist / script / template] for [job]." (Only if the artifact is real and you send it.)

## Contrarian and stake shapes

**Stop X, do Y**
"Stop [common advice] and start [named method] instead."
> Stop posting every day and start remixing your winners instead.

**Most people don't know**
"Most people don't know that [familiar thing] is [costing them / working against them]... [authority] actually confirmed this."
(Use a strong verb in the bracket. Keep the authority real.)

**Confirmed suspicion**
"[They were right all along / The rumors are true]. [Big name or group] [does the thing the viewer already suspected]."
The viewer's reaction has to be "I knew it", not "huh, interesting". The suspicion must be true or defensible. Accuse a category ("the old-school fitness gurus") rather than a named person you cannot back up.

**Paid vs free**
"My [service] charged $[X] a month. [This free thing] does all of it in [time]."
> My old coach charged $1,500 a month. This free weekly check does the same job in 5 minutes.

**Money humiliation**
"[Big name or group] just wasted $[X] on [thing] and got beaten by [underdog]."

**Blunt claim + proof number**
"[Claim]. [Number] in [timeframe]."
> I stopped running ads. 41 new clients in 90 days. (Made-up example; use your real number.)

## Curiosity shapes

**Paradox**
"[Concrete true thing] + but + [surprising stake or number nobody would guess]."
> I posted the same video four times. The fourth one booked more calls than the other three combined.

**Curiosity question**
"What happens when you [do slightly absurd real thing]?" / "Why does [concrete thing] [surprising result]?"

**Tried it myself**
"What happens when you [try the thing]? I tried it, and [number result]."

**Quoted moment** (story and connection posts)
"[Person] [texted / asked / told] me [quoted line] this morning..." then the facts, stated flat.

## Loop endings (stitch onto any hook)

- "...and almost everyone misses the [third / last] one. So let me show you what it is."
- "...and it's why [pain]. So let me show you."
- "...but there's one part nobody talks about."
- "...and the last one is the reason [pain]."
- "Here's exactly what I do."
- "Let me show you so you can steal it."

Avoid cheesy loops ("this one weird trick"). Make the loop specific to the video.

## Diagnosing a weak hook: the six slots

When a hook feels flat but you cannot say why, check which slot is weak:

1. **Subject** who or what it is about (you, I, a name)
2. **Action** the verb. Strong verbs carry heat.
3. **End state** the result, as shocking and non-obvious as possible
4. **Contrast** what the viewer expects vs what is true
5. **Proof** (optional) why trust it. Can wait for beat 2.
6. **Time** (optional) days, minutes, weeks. Adds urgency.

Slots 1 to 4 are required. Name the weakest one and fix only that.
