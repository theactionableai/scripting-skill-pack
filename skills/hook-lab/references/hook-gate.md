# Hook gate

Run every rendition through this before showing it. BLOCK means fix it or drop it. WARN means say it in one line and let the user decide.

## BLOCK

1. **No open loop.** The hook makes a statement and leaves no question. The viewer has no reason to stay.
2. **Gives away the payoff.** The thing the video reveals is named in the hook. Anchors are numbers and stakes, never the answer. ("Three things, and most people miss the third" holds it. "Remix your winners" gives it away.)
3. **Made-up facts.** Any number, result or personal story that is not true for the user. Use a real number or count something real.
4. **The stake gets defused.** A reassuring clause that cancels the tension ("but don't worry, it's easy"). If the easy part matters, flip it into curiosity ("and it takes ten minutes, which is why nobody does it").
5. **The first 10 words are runway.** "So one thing I've noticed lately is..." Read only the first 10 words. Would a stranger stop? If not, front-load it.
6. **Stumbles out loud.** Read it at talking speed. If the mouth trips, fix it.

## WARN

7. **No number.** Allowed, but say so. Most winners have one.
8. **External, not aspirational.** "Every coach who..." when it could be "If you want...".
9. **Safe down the middle.** Could a neutral news account say this line? Then it has no take. Push it 10% too far; the user can pull it back.
10. **Weak verbs.** "Shared, released, announced, posted, said." Prefer verbs with heat: wasted, missed, killed, buried, stole, blew.
11. **Blanket statement.** "Content is changing." If any account could say it, it is not a hook.
12. **Jargon.** Would a twelve-year-old know every word in half a second? Swap the niche word for the plain one.
13. **Bolted-on ending.** The second half feels like filler ("because viral videos with no leads are just entertainment"). Every clause has to earn its spot.
14. **Cliche loop.** "This one simple trick nobody knows." Make the loop specific to this video.
15. **Too long for one breath.** Under 10 seconds spoken. Shorter wins when it keeps the four parts.

## One-line verdict

After the gate, give one honest sentence: would this sit next to the user's best hooks in `winners.md`, and what is the weakest part? No point scores.
