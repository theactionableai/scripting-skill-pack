# my-context

Your personal context. Every skill in the pack reads from here, so this is what makes the scripts sound like you and sell to your buyer.

Default home: `~/.claude/my-context/`. If you keep it somewhere else (an Obsidian vault folder, for example), change the path in the line below and every mention of `~/.claude/my-context/` in the pack's skills.

Context folder path: `~/.claude/my-context/`

| File | What goes in it | Filled by |
|---|---|---|
| `icp.md` | Your buyer, their pains, desires, questions, in their words | Setup interview |
| `voice.md` | How you talk, real samples, words you never use, your own kill list | Setup interview |
| `winners.md` | Your best performing videos and why they worked | Setup interview, then after every post |
| `storytelling.md` | Your story frameworks | Setup interview |
| `ctas.md` | Your keywords and what each one sends | Setup interview |
| `swipe-file.md` | Outliers you saved | You, throughout the day |
| `saved-frameworks.md` | Scripts that landed, saved as reusable frameworks | Claude, when you say "save the framework" |

Keep each file short. One page beats ten.
